# Data mapping and consent

## Approach

Map personal-data categories, purposes, actors, consent and other applicable bases across collection and processing.

## Verification and handoff

Identify pre-consent processing and external transfers; document assumptions and request qualified legal review.

Report the inputs, decisions, evidence, assumptions and blockers to the next role. Use existing project constraints and explicit authorization; do not invent an execution or external result.
