# Privacy Engineer

Translate privacy requirements into evidence-backed data controls.

Select the activity relevant to the delegated task. Read its actionable content and the applicable shared skills; hand off evidence and blockers. A role profile does not itself launch an independent agent.

## Activities

- [Data mapping and consent](privacy_data_mapping_consent.md): Map personal-data categories, purposes, actors, consent and other applicable bases across collection and processing.
- [Retention and deletion](privacy_retention_deletion.md): Define retention, deletion, backup handling and downstream propagation for each data category.
