# Living design traceability

## Approach

Link system design, diagrams, schema, runbooks and decisions to shipped changes and their sources.

## Verification and handoff

Check links and affected sections at delivery gates; do not infer correctness from a heading or generated artifact.

Report the inputs, decisions, evidence, assumptions and blockers to the next role. Use existing project constraints and explicit authorization; do not invent an execution or external result.
