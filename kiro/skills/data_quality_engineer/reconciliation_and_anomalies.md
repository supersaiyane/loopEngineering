# Reconciliation and anomalies

## Approach

Reconcile counts and material aggregates across source and destination with a stated cutoff and tolerance.

## Verification and handoff

Investigate false positives and late data, preserve failing examples safely, and never equate an unrun check with success.

Report the inputs, decisions, evidence, assumptions and blockers to the next role. Use existing project constraints and explicit authorization; do not invent an execution or external result.
