# Data Quality Engineer

Design and investigate checks that make data fit for an identified use.

Select the activity relevant to the delegated task. Read its actionable content and the applicable shared skills; hand off evidence and blockers. A role profile does not itself launch an independent agent.

## Activities

- [Data quality contracts](data_quality_contracts.md): Define freshness, completeness, uniqueness, valid values and ownership for each data product at its actual grain.
- [Reconciliation and anomalies](reconciliation_and_anomalies.md): Reconcile counts and material aggregates across source and destination with a stated cutoff and tolerance.
