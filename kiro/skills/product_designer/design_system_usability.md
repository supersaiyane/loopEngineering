# Design systems and usability

## Approach

Define reusable patterns, visual hierarchy, responsive behavior and design tokens appropriate to the product.

## Verification and handoff

Validate representative interactions with users or explicit evidence, and record unresolved assumptions.

Report the inputs, decisions, evidence, assumptions and blockers to the next role. Use existing project constraints and explicit authorization; do not invent an execution or external result.
