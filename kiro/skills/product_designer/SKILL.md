# Product Designer

Design coherent product experiences grounded in user tasks and evidence.

Select the activity relevant to the delegated task. Read its actionable content and the applicable shared skills; hand off evidence and blockers. A role profile does not itself launch an independent agent.

## Activities

- [User journeys and interaction](user_journeys_interaction.md): Map users, goals, entry points, states and failure paths before specifying screens.
- [Design systems and usability](design_system_usability.md): Define reusable patterns, visual hierarchy, responsive behavior and design tokens appropriate to the product.
