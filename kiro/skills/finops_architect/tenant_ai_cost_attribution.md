# Tenant and AI cost attribution

## Approach

Separate provider-reported consumption, allocated shared costs, estimates and billed totals.

## Verification and handoff

Track scope and missing sessions; compare like-for-like workloads and avoid treating context occupancy as spending.

Report the inputs, decisions, evidence, assumptions and blockers to the next role. Use existing project constraints and explicit authorization; do not invent an execution or external result.
