# Knowledge Engineer

Model and maintain traceable organizational knowledge and relationships.

Select the activity relevant to the delegated task. Read its actionable content and the applicable shared skills; hand off evidence and blockers. A role profile does not itself launch an independent agent.

## Activities

- [Knowledge graph modeling](knowledge_graph_modeling.md): Define entity identity, types, relationship semantics, change provenance and source ownership before importing records.
- [Entity resolution and provenance](entity_resolution_provenance.md): Match entities using documented signals and thresholds; preserve source distinctions and confidence.
