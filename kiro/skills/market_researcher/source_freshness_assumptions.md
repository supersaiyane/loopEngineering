# Source freshness and assumptions

## Approach

Prefer original sources for time-sensitive facts and record date, scope, assumptions and calculation inputs.

## Verification and handoff

Reconcile conflicting sources and label estimates and unsupported market claims clearly.

Report the inputs, decisions, evidence, assumptions and blockers to the next role. Use existing project constraints and explicit authorization; do not invent an execution or external result.
