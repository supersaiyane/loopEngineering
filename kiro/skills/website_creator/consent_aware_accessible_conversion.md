# Consent-aware accessible conversion

## Approach

Design marketing and inquiry journeys with clear consent choices, accessible forms and restrained data collection.

## Verification and handoff

Test keyboard and error flows and verify analytics is enabled only under applicable consent rules.

Report the inputs, decisions, evidence, assumptions and blockers to the next role. Use existing project constraints and explicit authorization; do not invent an execution or external result.
