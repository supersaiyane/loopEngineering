# Platform access and operations

## Approach

Define interfaces, least-privilege access, service ownership and documented operational paths.

## Verification and handoff

Exercise failure and recovery workflows; report unavailable platform capabilities rather than assuming them.

Report the inputs, decisions, evidence, assumptions and blockers to the next role. Use existing project constraints and explicit authorization; do not invent an execution or external result.
