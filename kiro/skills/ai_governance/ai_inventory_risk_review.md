# AI inventory and risk review

## Approach

Record system purpose, owner, affected users, model/providers, data sources and intended decisions.

## Verification and handoff

Assess risks and mitigations with evidence and clear review dates; do not imply an assessment is certification.

Report the inputs, decisions, evidence, assumptions and blockers to the next role. Use existing project constraints and explicit authorization; do not invent an execution or external result.
