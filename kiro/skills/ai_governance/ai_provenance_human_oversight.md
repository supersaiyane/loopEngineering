# AI provenance and human oversight

## Approach

Trace source data, model versions and decision points to the responsible reviewer.

## Verification and handoff

Define escalation and correction paths for high-impact outcomes and document gaps or unavailable provenance.

Report the inputs, decisions, evidence, assumptions and blockers to the next role. Use existing project constraints and explicit authorization; do not invent an execution or external result.
