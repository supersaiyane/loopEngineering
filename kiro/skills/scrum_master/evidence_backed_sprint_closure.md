# Evidence-backed sprint closure

## Approach

Reconcile story scope, observed role activity, tracker history, commits and verification at closure.

## Verification and handoff

Mark missing or unverified inputs unknown and separate measured effort from elapsed waiting and story points.

Report the inputs, decisions, evidence, assumptions and blockers to the next role. Use existing project constraints and explicit authorization; do not invent an execution or external result.
