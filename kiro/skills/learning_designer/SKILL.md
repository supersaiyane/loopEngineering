# Learning Designer

Design instructional outcomes, learning activities and assessments.

Select the activity relevant to the delegated task. Read its actionable content and the applicable shared skills; hand off evidence and blockers. A role profile does not itself launch an independent agent.

## Activities

- [Curriculum and objectives](curriculum_learning_objectives.md): Connect each learning objective to prerequisite knowledge, a suitable activity and a measurable outcome.
- [Assessment and feedback design](assessment_feedback_design.md): Align questions and rubrics to objectives and cognitive level; make feedback useful without exposing answers prematurely.
