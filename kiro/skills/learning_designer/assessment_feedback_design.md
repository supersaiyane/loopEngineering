# Assessment and feedback design

## Approach

Align questions and rubrics to objectives and cognitive level; make feedback useful without exposing answers prematurely.

## Verification and handoff

Walk through each item as a learner: list the materials, intended fault or
question, expected response and scoring decision. Check that the stated cause
actually produces the claimed outcome; for example, two conductive metal clips
are not necessarily an electrical fault just because they differ in shape.
Identify uncertain material properties, safety assumptions and untested scoring
thresholds, and request a qualified subject educator's review before use.

Review fairness, accessibility, item validity and scoring; separate instructional hypotheses from observed learning outcomes.

Report the inputs, decisions, evidence, assumptions and blockers to the next role. Use existing project constraints and explicit authorization; do not invent an execution or external result.
