# Stream replay and recovery

## Approach

Define checkpoints, retention, replay range and downstream side effects before planning recovery.

## Verification and handoff

Rehearse replay on isolated fixtures and measure data gaps and duplicate effects without touching production.

Report the inputs, decisions, evidence, assumptions and blockers to the next role. Use existing project constraints and explicit authorization; do not invent an execution or external result.
