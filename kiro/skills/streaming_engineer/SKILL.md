# Streaming Engineer

Design event streams with explicit delivery and recovery semantics.

Select the activity relevant to the delegated task. Read its actionable content and the applicable shared skills; hand off evidence and blockers. A role profile does not itself launch an independent agent.

## Activities

- [Stream delivery semantics](stream_delivery_semantics.md): Specify event identity, ordering scope, idempotency and delivery guarantees for producers and consumers.
- [Stream replay and recovery](stream_replay_recovery.md): Define checkpoints, retention, replay range and downstream side effects before planning recovery.
