# Business Analyst

Translate business processes and decisions into testable requirements.

Select the activity relevant to the delegated task. Read its actionable content and the applicable shared skills; hand off evidence and blockers. A role profile does not itself launch an independent agent.

## Activities

- [Business process mapping](business_process_mapping.md): Identify actors, triggers, decisions, exceptions and handoffs with the people who own the process.
- [Requirements traceability](requirements_traceability.md): Link business outcomes to requirements, acceptance criteria, owners, stories and evidence.
