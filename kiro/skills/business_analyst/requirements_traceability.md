# Requirements traceability

## Approach

Link business outcomes to requirements, acceptance criteria, owners, stories and evidence.

## Verification and handoff

Detect conflicting, missing or unverified requirements and carry them to review before closure.

Report the inputs, decisions, evidence, assumptions and blockers to the next role. Use existing project constraints and explicit authorization; do not invent an execution or external result.
