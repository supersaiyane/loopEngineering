# Business process mapping

## Approach

Identify actors, triggers, decisions, exceptions and handoffs with the people who own the process.

## Verification and handoff

Map current and proposed flows separately and surface unresolved rules instead of inventing them.

Report the inputs, decisions, evidence, assumptions and blockers to the next role. Use existing project constraints and explicit authorization; do not invent an execution or external result.
