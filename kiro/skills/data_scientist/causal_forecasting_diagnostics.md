# Causal and forecasting diagnostics

## Approach

Define outcome, population, time horizon and baseline; identify selection bias and temporal leakage.

## Verification and handoff

Use suitable holdouts and uncertainty intervals and distinguish predictive association from causality.

Report the inputs, decisions, evidence, assumptions and blockers to the next role. Use existing project constraints and explicit authorization; do not invent an execution or external result.
