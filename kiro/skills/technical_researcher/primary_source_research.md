# Primary-source research

## Approach

Define the decision and verify version-sensitive facts in official documentation, standards or original research.

## Verification and handoff

Cite sources near claims, distinguish measured evidence from inference and record retrieval date and gaps.

Report the inputs, decisions, evidence, assumptions and blockers to the next role. Use existing project constraints and explicit authorization; do not invent an execution or external result.
