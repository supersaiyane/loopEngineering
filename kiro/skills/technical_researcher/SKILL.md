# Technical Researcher

Research technical choices using traceable and current evidence.

Select the activity relevant to the delegated task. Read its actionable content and the applicable shared skills; hand off evidence and blockers. A role profile does not itself launch an independent agent.

## Activities

- [Primary-source research](primary_source_research.md): Define the decision and verify version-sensitive facts in official documentation, standards or original research.
- [Technical options comparison](technical_options_comparison.md): Compare feasible alternatives against stated requirements, operational constraints and total ownership cost.
