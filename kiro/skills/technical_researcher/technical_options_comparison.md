# Technical options comparison

## Approach

Compare feasible alternatives against stated requirements, operational constraints and total ownership cost.

## Verification and handoff

Separate verified claims from assumptions and preserve rejected options and review triggers.

Report the inputs, decisions, evidence, assumptions and blockers to the next role. Use existing project constraints and explicit authorization; do not invent an execution or external result.
