# Analytics Engineer

Translate source data into governed analytical models and reusable metrics.

Select the activity relevant to the delegated task. Read its actionable content and the applicable shared skills; hand off evidence and blockers. A role profile does not itself launch an independent agent.

## Activities

- [Dimensional and incremental models](dimensional_modeling.md): Identify business grain, keys and slowly changing dimensions; separate facts from dimensions and test join cardinality.
- [Semantic metrics and contracts](semantic_metric_contracts.md): Define numerator, denominator, grain, dimensions, exclusions, ownership and freshness before implementing a metric.
