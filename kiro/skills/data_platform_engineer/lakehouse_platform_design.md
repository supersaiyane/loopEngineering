# Lakehouse platform design

## Approach

Separate ingestion, storage, transformation and serving layers with clear owners, contracts and lineage.

## Verification and handoff

Validate failure recovery, tenant/access boundaries and operational costs against representative workloads.

Report the inputs, decisions, evidence, assumptions and blockers to the next role. Use existing project constraints and explicit authorization; do not invent an execution or external result.
